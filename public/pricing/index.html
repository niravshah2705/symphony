Pricing questions

How is Archagentix priced?
Per seat per month. Pilot is free, Team is $49 per seat per month, and Enterprise is custom-priced for dedicated deployments and governance requirements.
Do agents need access to my raw API keys?
No. Credentials live in the per-organization encrypted vault or platform-managed storage. An egress proxy sidecar injects the real credential on outbound calls, so planner and coder containers never hold raw secrets.
Can I bring my own model keys?
Yes. Team and Enterprise plans support per-organization customer keys for LLM providers, GitHub, and Linear.
Is there a discount for annual billing?
Annual billing saves two months versus monthly. Contact archagentix@gmail.com for invoicing and Enterprise terms.
