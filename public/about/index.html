About Company

Archagentix builds the discipline for shipping with AI agents.

Archagentix Private Limited, based in Pune, Maharashtra, is the creator of the Agentic Development Life Cycle (ADLC) — a human-governed operating model in which AI agents plan, implement, test, review, and deliver software while people own approvals, policy, and outcomes.

What we believe

  • Agents should work in isolated environments that never hold raw credentials.
  • Every stage must leave durable, inspectable evidence — plans, tests, diffs, review decisions.
  • Humans stay accountable: review gates are a feature, not friction.
  • The lifecycle is teachable: we publish ADLC openly through the overview and the full brief.
