## 1. Mode of delivery

All deliverables are electronic and are made available to you in your Archagentix
workspace or by email to the address on your account. There is no physical
shipment, no customs declaration, and no delivery address required to buy or use
the service. Where a carrier would normally be named, the answer is: nothing is
carried.

## 2. What is delivered, and how fast

Timelines are measured from the moment payment is captured, not from the moment an
order form is signed, unless a row says otherwise. "Business days" means Monday to
Friday, excluding public holidays in Pune, Maharashtra.

- **Workspace access, monthly Pilot or Team plan.** Provisioned automatically and
  immediately. Sign-in becomes available within **5 minutes** of a successful
  payment, and the welcome email with your workspace address follows within
  **1 business day**.
- **Additional seats on an existing plan.** Provisioned immediately; the new seat is
  usable as soon as the payment is captured.
- **Prepaid usage credits and auto-recharge top-ups.** Credited to the organization
  wallet **instantly**, in the same transaction that charges you.
- **Annual subscription.** Same as monthly: workspace access within **5 minutes** of
  capture, seats available immediately.
- **API keys and integration tokens.** Minted by you in **Settings → Credentials**
  the moment your workspace exists. There is no separate issue request and no
  waiting period. Keys for an Enterprise deployment are issued in your dedicated
  workspace within **2 business days** of that workspace being stood up.
- **Invoices and credit notes.** Delivered by email to the billing address within
  **2 business days** of the charge or of the refund decision.
- **Enterprise and dedicated deployments.** The workspace for a dedicated
  deployment is provisioned within **10 business days** of a signed order form and
  the first payment, or within the date written in the order form, whichever is
  earlier. Repository connections, identity providers and network access are
  configured in that window as long as you supply the required approvals.
- **Support response, not a deliverable.** First response within **1 business day**
  for Pilot, **4 business hours** for Team, and per the service levels in your order
  form for Enterprise.

## 3. What can delay delivery

Delivery is not late, for the purpose of this policy or a refund claim, when the
delay is caused by:

- a payment that has not actually settled, including a pending bank transfer or a
  charge your issuer placed on hold;
- your email provider rejecting or quarantining our messages, which is why the
  workspace address also appears on the confirmation page;
- a missing approval or credential on your side, most often a repository permission,
  an identity-provider consent, or a firewall rule blocking our egress ranges;
- a domain or DNS change still propagating;
- an event outside our reasonable control.

We will tell you what we need in order to finish provisioning, and the clock resumes
when we have it.

## 4. Proof of delivery

For a digital service, delivery is evidenced by records rather than a courier
receipt. The following constitute proof that we delivered:

- the payment capture record and its timestamp;
- the creation timestamp of your organization and workspace;
- the activation timestamp of each seat, and the audit entry for each API key mint;
- the send log for the welcome email and for each invoice.

You can see the seat and key audit entries yourself in your workspace. If you need
them for a dispute, request them from
[support@archagentix.com](mailto:support@archagentix.com) and we provide the relevant
extract within **5 business days**.

## 5. If delivery does not happen

- **Workspace not available 1 business day after payment.** Write to
  [support@archagentix.com](mailto:support@archagentix.com) with the payment
  reference. We resolve it within **2 business days**.
- **Still not delivered 5 business days after a clean order** with nothing outstanding
  on your side: you may end the order and receive a **full refund** of the
  undelivered amount, on top of the eligibility in the
  [Refund and Cancellation Policy](#/refund).
- **A seat that cannot be assigned** because of a defect on our side is not billed
  for the period it was unusable.
