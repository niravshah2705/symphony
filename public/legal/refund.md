## 1. What you are buying

Archagentix sells hosted access to subscription software. There is no physical
goods shipment. A paid subscription grants a number of seats, each seat being one
named user in your organization's workspace, for the billing term you selected.
Usage-based charges for model calls, if separately enabled on your plan, are billed
as described in your order form and are consumed as incurred.

## 2. How billing works

- **Monthly plans** renew automatically on the calendar day your subscription
  started, each month, until you cancel.
- **Annual plans** renew automatically once per year and are charged for the full
  year up front. Annual pricing already reflects a discount versus twelve monthly
  payments.
- You are charged at the start of each term. An invoice that fails is retried, and
  the workspace is not suspended before we have contacted you.
- All prices shown on the pricing page are the prices in effect at the time of your
  order. A price change does not apply to a term you have already paid for.

## 3. Cancelling your subscription

You may cancel at any time. Cancelling stops the **next** renewal; it does not
delete your workspace or end the term you have already paid for.

- **How to cancel:** from **Settings → Billing** in your workspace, or by email to
  [support@archagentix.com](mailto:support@archagentix.com) from the address on the
  account. We acknowledge a cancellation request by email within **2 business days**.
- **When it takes effect:** at the end of the current billing term. Your seats stay
  usable until the term expires.
- **No minimum commitment** applies to the Pilot or monthly Team plans. Enterprise
  terms, including any committed period, are the ones written in your order form.
- **Downgrades.** Reducing your seat count takes effect at the next renewal; we do
  not pro-rate a reduction in the middle of a paid term.

## 4. Refund eligibility

Refunds are issued for the following situations. Amounts are returned for the
unused portion of the term unless stated otherwise.

- **First purchase, any paid plan: 14 days.** If you cancel within 14 calendar days
  of the first charge on a new subscription, we refund that charge in full. This
  applies once per organization.
- **Duplicate or erroneous charge.** Refunded in full. This includes a charge made
  twice for the same term, or a charge that continued after a cancellation we
  accepted.
- **Material service failure.** If the platform is unavailable beyond the
  availability commitment in your order form, or a defect makes a core capability
  unusable and we cannot restore it within 30 calendar days of your report, you may
  end the subscription and receive a pro-rata refund of the unused term.
- **Price or plan change applied wrongly.** Refunded as a pro-rata credit or cash
  refund, your choice.
- **Annual renewal charged after a valid cancellation request.** Refunded in full.

Refunds are **not** issued for:

- Used portions of a term, including a term you simply decide to leave early.
- Usage-based charges for model calls and other third-party compute already
  consumed on your behalf.
- Mid-term seat reductions, which take effect at the next renewal instead.
- A renewal on an annual plan more than 14 days after the charge, provided the
  reminder email we send before renewal reached the address on the account.
- Free Pilot usage; there is nothing to refund.

## 5. Refund timelines

- Submit the request to [support@archagentix.com](mailto:support@archagentix.com)
  with your organization name and the invoice or transaction reference.
- We acknowledge within **2 business days** and give a decision within **5 business
  days** of receiving the information we need from you.
- An approved refund is issued to the **original payment method**. Credits are not
  substituted for cash refunds unless you ask for one.
- Card issuers and wallets apply their own settlement time. Expect the credit to
  appear within **5 to 10 business days** of us issuing it, and up to one full
  billing cycle in rare cases.
- For an internationally processed payment, the amount returned is the captured
  amount in the currency you were charged. Any currency difference caused by your
  issuer is outside our control.

## 6. Chargebacks

We ask that you contact us before disputing a charge. If you open a chargeback while
a refund request from you is already under review, we will respond to the issuer with
the usage and delivery records for the subscription, including provisioning time,
seat activity and cancellation date. A chargeback filed while a refund we already
approved is in transit will be disputed.

## 7. Data after cancellation

Your workspace remains exportable for **30 calendar days** after the term ends, so you
can take your repositories, tickets and run history with you. After that window the
data of a cancelled organization is deleted on the schedule described in the
[Privacy Notice](#/privacy). Deletion is irreversible, so export first.

## 8. Changes to this policy

This policy governs orders placed while it is the published version. A revised
version applies to renewals after its effective date, and we email the account
address at least **15 days** before a change to refund eligibility takes effect for
you.
