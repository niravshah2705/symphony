About Us

Who we are

Archagentix Private Limited builds the Agentic Development Life Cycle (ADLC) — a platform where planner and coder deep agents ship software inside your own project, with a human approving every step. Agents run isolated, hold no raw credentials, and leave an evidence trail you can audit.

Both product teams and enterprises running regulated codebases use ADLC to put agents into real delivery pipelines without giving up control.

  • Agents in the loop, humans in control
  • Egress isolation — agents never hold raw keys
  • Audit-ready evidence for every change
  • Deploy in our cloud or your own GCP project
  • Linear-native planning and workflow
  • Transparent, fixed-price plans
  • We listen, we understand, we ship solutions
