Archagentix supplies digital services only. Nothing in this policy involves a physical parcel, and no shipping carrier is involved at any stage. This policy states what is delivered, how fast, and what to do when delivery does not happen. It is incorporated into and forms part of the Terms of Service and the Refund and Cancellation Policy.

1. Mode of delivery

All deliverables are electronic and are made available to you in your Archagentix workspace or by email to the address on your account. There is no physical shipment, no customs declaration, and no delivery address required to buy or use the service. Where a carrier would normally be named, the answer is: nothing is carried.

2. What is delivered, and how fast

Timelines are measured from the moment payment is captured, not from the moment an order form is signed, unless a row says otherwise. "Business days" means Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in Pune, Maharashtra.

  • Workspace access, monthly Pilot or Team plan. Provisioned automatically and immediately. Sign-in becomes available within 5 minutes of a successful payment, and the welcome email with your workspace address follows within 1 business day.
  • Additional seats on an existing plan. Provisioned immediately; the new seat is usable as soon as the payment is captured.
  • Prepaid usage credits and auto-recharge top-ups. Credited to the organization wallet instantly, in the same transaction that charges you.
  • Annual subscription. Same as monthly: workspace access within 5 minutes of capture, seats available immediately.
  • API keys and integration tokens. Minted by you in Settings → Credentials the moment your workspace exists. There is no separate issue request and no waiting period. Keys for an Enterprise deployment are issued in your dedicated workspace within 2 business days of that workspace being stood up.
  • Invoices and credit notes. Delivered by email to the billing address within 2 business days of the charge or of the refund decision.
  • Enterprise and dedicated deployments. The workspace for a dedicated deployment is provisioned within 10 business days of a signed order form and the first payment, or within the date written in the order form, whichever is earlier. Repository connections, identity providers and network access are configured in that window as long as you supply the required approvals.
  • Support response, not a deliverable. First response within 1 business day for Pilot, 4 business hours for Team, and per the service levels in your order form for Enterprise.

3. What can delay delivery

Delivery is not late, for the purpose of this policy or a refund claim, when the delay is caused by:

  • a payment that has not actually settled, including a pending bank transfer or a charge your issuer placed on hold;
  • your email provider rejecting or quarantining our messages, which is why the workspace address also appears on the confirmation page;
  • a missing approval or credential on your side, most often a repository permission, an identity-provider consent, or a firewall rule blocking our egress ranges;
  • a domain or DNS change still propagating;
  • an event outside our reasonable control.

We will tell you what we need in order to finish provisioning, and the clock resumes when we have it.

4. Proof of delivery

For a digital service, delivery is evidenced by records rather than a courier receipt. The following constitute proof that we delivered:

  • the payment capture record and its timestamp;
  • the creation timestamp of your organization and workspace;
  • the activation timestamp of each seat, and the audit entry for each API key mint;
  • the send log for the welcome email and for each invoice.

You can see the seat and key audit entries yourself in your workspace. If you need them for a dispute, request them from support@archagentix.com and we provide the relevant extract within 5 business days.

5. If delivery does not happen

  • Workspace not available 1 business day after payment. Write to support@archagentix.com with the payment reference. We resolve it within 2 business days.
  • Still not delivered 5 business days after a clean order with nothing outstanding on your side: you may end the order and receive a full refund of the undelivered amount, on top of the eligibility in the Refund and Cancellation Policy.
  • A seat that cannot be assigned because of a defect on our side is not billed for the period it was unusable.

6. Taxes and statutory documents

Prices are quoted exclusive of Indian GST and of any withholding or import-style tax applicable in your country, unless a plan page says the price is inclusive. Where GST applies, it is charged and shown on the invoice under Archagentix Private Limited's registration, GSTIN 27ABGCA7851D1ZN (Maharashtra). The invoice we issue carries the GST treatment for your billing address. For an Indian business, supply is treated as the online provision of a service in the State of Maharashtra, place of supply per the Integrated GST Act where the recipient is outside Maharashtra. For customers outside India, no Indian GST is charged on the invoice and you are responsible for any local tax or reverse charge in your own jurisdiction. We do not ship a physical tax document; the electronic invoice is the statutory document.
